## Introduction
Two years ago, I started my first professional journey at Perhutani.
At the time, I didn't really know what to expect. I was entering a professional environment for the first time, learning how an organization works, how people collaborate, and how to deal with real-world problems.
Before getting into my experience, here's a little context about the company.

## What is Perhutani?

![perhutani-explanation]({{ '/assets/perhutani/perhutani.png' | relative_url }})
Perhutani is a state-owned company responsible for managing forests and operating in the forestry sector.

Before I got my full-time job there, I was doing a 6 Months high school program where, during the third year, students had to do an internship based on their selected major. At the time, my major was Accounting, so I thought choosing Perhutani was a great choice.

During my internship program, I gained a lot of experience that I could not learn from school, especially about communication, teamwork, responsibility, and how a real workplace operates.

## Career Path
My internship lasted for six months. Here's a little breakdown of each path I took.

### Planning Department
I worked in the Planning Department for four months. There, I got my first experience in a professional work environment. 
During this periods at Planning Department i've got a lots experiences, for example. For every single teak harvesting the data core about the woods is created at `Planning Department`.

## Project
#### Datasearch Perencanaan

:::caution[Problem]
Searching through large amounts of planning data manually was time-consuming and inefficient.
:::

:::note[Result]
Built a search tool that makes planning data easier to find by filtering based on BKPH Name and ID Number.
:::

Created with tech stacks: **Next.js, Spreadsheet, Tailwind CSS**

### Archive Department
After my time in the Planning Department, I was assigned to the Archive Department, where I was responsible for organizing and managing archived documents.

My responsibilities included sorting documents, applying official stamps, and ensuring documents were properly organized and ready for archival.

## What's Next?

The six-month internship eventually came to an end, but my journey at Perhutani didn't.

After completing my internship, I was given the opportunity to continue working at Perhutani as a full-time employee.

What started as an internship became the beginning of a longer professional journey, where I would take on new responsibilities, work with different departments, and explore how technology could be used to solve problems in my day-to-day work.
And that's where the next chapter begins.

I started in the Sarana & Prasarana, IT & Opset department at `June 2025`, where I worked as an IT Support. This was my first experience handling IT-related responsibilities in a professional environment. During my time there, i also building applications to support my department

## Projects
### Opsetku
<img src="{{ '/assets/perhutani/sarpra_p1.jpeg' | relative_url }}" alt="Sarpra Project 1" width="200">

Opsetku was responsible for managing rental payments data for houses owned by Perhutani.

:::caution[Problem]
Before that applications introduced, keeping track of which tenants had paid and which payments were still outstanding was difficult to manage manually with papers.
:::

:::note[Result]
Built an internal application that allows the team to easily manage and track which tenants have paid and which payments are still outstanding. The application also provides a printing feature to generate physical payment records when needed.
:::

Created with tech stacks: **Jetpack Compose, Android Studio**
